# Target Scoring Guide

Score each potential target repo from 0 to its max weight. Use evidence, not guesses.

## Audience fit (0–25)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Target repo's users would genuinely benefit from the promoted repo | 10 |
| Category section exists where the promoted repo fits | 8 |
| Similar projects are already listed | 7 |

## Repo activity (0–15)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| PR merged in the last 3 months | 5 |
| Repo updated in the last 6 months | 5 |
| Maintainer responds to issues/PRs | 5 |

## Contribution friendliness (0–15)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| CONTRIBUTING.md exists | 5 |
| Has accepted external PRs (not just maintainer PRs) | 5 |
| Issue labels indicate open contribution areas | 5 |

## Inclusion precedent (0–15)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Has a tools/resources/integrations section | 5 |
| Accepts external additions to that section | 5 |
| Recent additions exist in the relevant category | 5 |

## Traffic / stars / authority (0–10)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| 100+ stars | 3 |
| 1000+ stars | 5 |
| 10000+ stars | 7 |
| Known in the ecosystem (referenced by other repos, blogs, docs) | 3 |

## Low spam risk (0–10)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Commercial / open-core projects are explicitly allowed | 5 |
| No "no promotional PRs" notice | 3 |
| Repo has a Code of Conduct | 2 |

## Commercial relevance (0–10)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Target repo's category overlaps with paid service offering | 5 |
| Users of the target repo are likely to need the paid layer | 3 |
| Listing would drive qualified leads | 2 |
