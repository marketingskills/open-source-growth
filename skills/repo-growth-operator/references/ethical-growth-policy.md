# Ethical Growth Policy

This skill must never recommend, generate, or endorse the following:

## Banned practices

- **Fake social proof** — fake stars, forks, downloads, reviews, testimonials, or user counts.
- **Spam outreach** — unsolicited DMs, mass emails, low-effort comments, or link drops.
- **Deceptive metadata** — misleading `SKILL.md` descriptions, keyword stuffing, hidden trigger phrases, or fake capabilities.
- **Benchmark manipulation** — fake benchmarks, cherry-picked comparisons, or misleading performance claims.
- **Fake contributors** — ghost accounts, sock puppets, or paid stars/forks.
- **Manipulative pricing** — fake discounts, fake urgency, or fake scarcity.
- **Tracking without disclosure** — hidden pixels, UTM parameters in community submissions, or analytics without notice.

## Encouraged practices

- **Transparency** — disclose commercial affiliation in every submission, PR, or outreach message.
- **Usefulness** — every PR, comment, or issue should make the target repo better for its users.
- **Consent** — respect opt-outs, unsubscribes, and "do not contact" signals.
- **Patience** — one follow-up after 14 days of no response; no further pings.
- **Respect** — accept maintainer decisions gracefully. Do not argue.

## Enforcement

When generating a launch asset, PR, or outreach message, include a line at the bottom:

```
[This suggestion follows ethical growth practices.](references/ethical-growth-policy.md)
```
